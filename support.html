Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a deck?

Open the Decks section and tap the "+" button.

How do I import a decklist?

Use the Import Deck option and paste a supported decklist.

How do I restore Premium purchases?

Open the Premium screen and tap "Restore Purchases".
